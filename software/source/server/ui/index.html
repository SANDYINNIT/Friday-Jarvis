    CONVERSATION
    Personality & initiative
    Dry wit when welcome. Clear answers when it matters.
    For the user’s private text on this window only. Light or Spicy makes these replies text-only. This is your choice, not identity verification. Voice, Telegram, n8n and notifications stay neutral. Say “stop joking” to turn banter off.
    Owner decisions and open priority tasks can add a quiet Inbox suggestion, at most once per ten minutes and eight per day per session. A suggestion never approves or executes an action. Off does not cancel reminders you requested.
    Reading preferences…
    Expression & scope
    Focused, amused and concerned are wording cues, not subjective feelings. No psychological profile is created. Preferences are saved on this computer; expression and suggestions last for this session.
    

    DESKTOP COMPANION
    Cursor buddy & top panel
    Keep FRIDAY beside your pointer, and open a compact control panel while the main window is hidden.
    Saved on this computer. These choices do not enable recording, screen capture or computer control.
    Hiding the app keeps FRIDAY running. A true Exit closes the buddy and top panel. Notifications and notes last for this session, up to 30 minutes; they are not automatically saved as memories or tasks.
    

    SAVED WINDOWS REGISTRATION
    Start at sign-in
    Applies at your next Windows sign-in. Saving does not launch or restart FRIDAY.

    Approval is one-use and bound to this action, its arguments, this account/session and expiry. It is not a Workspace review or an answer to the oldest pending question.
